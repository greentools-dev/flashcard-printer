Duplex Printing Guide & Frequently Asked Questions

How does column mirroring work?

In standard 2-column printing, when paper flips horizontally along its long edge, Column 1 on the front becomes Column 2 on the back. Our engine calculates this geometric transposition automatically: Column A on Page 1 aligns directly with Column B on Page 2.

Which printer setting should I choose?

In your browser's Print dialog, choose Two-Sided Printing with Flip on Long Edge (portrait orientation). Set margins to "Minimum" or "None" for edge-to-edge cutting.

What separator characters can I use?

You can separate questions and answers using a vertical pipe (|), a tab character (copy-pasted from Excel or Google Sheets), or a hyphen ( - ).

Is this tool private?

Yes, 100%. All flashcards, vocabulary decks, and confidential exam notes are stored strictly in your browser session. Zero data is ever logged or sent to any server.
